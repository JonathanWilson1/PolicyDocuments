AI Decorator Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

AI Decorator ("the app") is made by Jonathan Wilson, an independent developer in the United Kingdom ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the app handles and why. Questions: jonathan@podcastalarm.app.

Photos you choose to redesign

When you start a redesign, the photo you picked or took (and any mask you drew) is sent with your style choice or prompt to Replicate, which runs the image model (FLUX, by Black Forest Labs) and returns the result. Photos are used only to make your redesign. They are not used for advertising and I do not sell them.

The app only reads the photos you pick or take; it never scans your photo library. Camera and photo access can be turned off any time in iOS Settings.

Your redesign history

Before-and-after images and prompts are saved on your device so you can see them in the app's gallery. They are not uploaded to me. Deleting the app deletes them.

Analytics and crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config to understand how the app is used, fix crashes and switch features on or off. This includes which screens are opened, the text of prompts you type, ratings you give results, error messages, device model, OS version, language and approximate country. It is linked to a random app-instance identifier, not to your name or email. The app does not use this data for advertising or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. The app uses RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your App Store purchase receipts. I never see your payment details.

What I don't collect

No account, name, email address, contacts or precise location is collected by the app.

Retention and your rights

Firebase Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask for access to, or deletion of, data relating to you: email jonathan@podcastalarm.app. Because the data is not linked to your identity, include your app's anonymous ID if you have it, or the date and approximate time you used the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

AI Decorator Support

Need help? Email jonathan@podcastalarm.app and include your iPhone model and iOS version. I usually reply within two working days.

Common questions

My redesign failed or is taking a long time

Redesigns need an internet connection and normally take 10–40 seconds. If one fails, check your connection and try again. Very dark or blurry photos give weaker results.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open the subscription screen in the app and tap Restore, signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → AI Decorator and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. See Apple's guide.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by me. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my past redesigns?

In the gallery inside the app. They are stored only on your device, so they are not carried over to a new phone and are removed if you delete the app. Save the ones you want to keep to Photos with the share button.
